## Important source gate

The project brief requires the official *Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011* PDF as the authoritative legal source. That PDF was not present in the attached materials used to generate this package. Consequently, no legal rule numbers, thresholds, exceptions, or page citations have been fabricated; the legal documents provide a source-gated schema and activation workflow.
