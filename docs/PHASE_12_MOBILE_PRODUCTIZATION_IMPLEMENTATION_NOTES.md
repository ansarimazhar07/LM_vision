# LM-Vision Phase 12 — Mobile Productization & Legal Metrology Intelligence Implementation Notes

## Executive Summary

Phase 12 productizes the LM-Vision mobile client into a field-ready, production-grade inspection tool, enshrines the authoritative Legal Metrology Rules Library (GSR 202(E) 2011), and introduces explainable AI intelligence while strictly preserving the foundational statutory governance invariant:
1. **Local OCR/CV** $\rightarrow$ On-device observation.
2. **Gemini AI** $\rightarrow$ Advisory multimodal observation & explanation (Never statutory authority).
3. **Rule Engine** $\rightarrow$ Authoritative statutory assessment under GSR 202(E) (2011).
4. **Human Inspector** $\rightarrow$ Sole final legal decision maker under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009.

---

## Architecture & Implementation Overview

### 1. Mobile Authentication & Profile Governance (Sections A & B)
- **Authentication Screens & State**: Segmented `SIGN_IN`, `SIGN_UP`, and `FORGOT_PASSWORD` modes implemented in [`LoginScreen.tsx`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/apps/mobile/src/screens/LoginScreen.tsx) and wired to [`AuthProvider.tsx`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/apps/mobile/src/auth/AuthProvider.tsx).
- **Client-Side Validations**: Full name required on registration, password minimum length enforced at 8 characters, and password confirmation matching.
- **Demo Mode**: Explicitly separated from network authentication. Operates strictly in-memory with pre-configured inspector jurisdiction (`National Capital Region`), allowing instant evaluation without cloud dependencies.
- **Profile Screen**: Added [`ProfileScreen.tsx`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/apps/mobile/src/screens/ProfileScreen.tsx) rendering live identity, active AI engine mode, offline sync snapshot, active rule bundle identifier (`LM-IN-RULES-2026.09`), and inspector name updates.
- **Governance Lock**: Unilateral client-side role escalation is strictly locked to prevent unauthorized privilege tampering.

### 2. Field Inspection Dashboard (Section C)
- **Live Metrics**: [`HomeScreen.tsx`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/apps/mobile/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx) dynamically computes dashboard metrics from real in-memory drafts and completed inspection history:
  - Active draft case count (0 or 1).
  - Pending review queue.
  - Finalized cases count.
  - Today's inspections (completed in rolling 24-hour window).
  - Violations flagged across mandatory declarations, MRP, and net quantity.
  - Sync queue pending count & conflict indicator.
- **Zero-State Integrity**: When zero inspections exist, all counters honestly reflect 0 without synthetic vanity numbers.
- **Quick Actions**: One-touch routing to `[New Inspection]`, `[Scan Package]`, `[Rules Library]`, and `[Inspections & Reports]`.
- **Vision Engine Switcher**: Quick-toggle between `LOCAL_ONLY`, `HYBRID`, and `GEMINI_LIVE` engines with real-time UI indicator.

### 3. Legal Metrology Rules Library (Section D)
- **Authoritative Statutory Catalog**: Complete statutory metadata for all rules under the **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (G.S.R. 202(E))**:
  - Exact rule numbering (e.g. `Rule 6(1)(a)`, `Rule 6(1)(b)`, `Rule 6(1)(c)`, `Rule 6(1)(e)`, `Rule 12`, `Rule 18`).
  - Gazette provenance: `G.S.R. 202(E) dated 07.03.2011, effective 01.04.2011`.
  - Legislative authority: `Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution`.
  - Statutory parent act: `Legal Metrology Act, 2009`.
- **Statutory Separation**: Complete distinction between the Official Gazette legal text and the LM-Vision software implementation logic.
- **Offline Search & Category Filters**: [`RuleLibraryScreen.tsx`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/apps/mobile/src/screens/RuleLibraryScreen.tsx) supports real-time text query and chip filtering across `ALL`, `MANDATORY`, `MRP`, `NET_QTY`, `MFR`, and `ORIGIN`.
- **Detail Screen**: [`LegalRuleDetailScreen.tsx`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/apps/mobile/src/screens/LegalRuleDetailScreen.tsx) provides deep statutory citations, applicability matrices, and software validation rules.
- **Bundle Manifest**: Verified distribution bundle identifier `LM-IN-RULES-2026.09` with cryptographic manifest checksum verification.

### 4. Advisory AI Explanation & Guardrails (Section E)
- **Explain Assessment Card**: Integrated into [`FindingDetailScreen.tsx`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/apps/mobile/src/screens/FindingDetailScreen.tsx):
  - Direct `[View Rule in Library →]` button routing to the exact statutory section.
  - Observed packaging declaration vs. expected statutory value.
  - Advisory reasoning generated by Gemini AI with AI provider & model name clearly tagged.
  - Mandatory statutory disclaimer: *"Advisory analysis by LM-Vision AI Engine. Not a statutory legal decision. The Inspector retains final authority."*
- **Offline Deterministic Fallback**: When offline or in `LOCAL_ONLY` mode, explanations fall back deterministically to the local rule bundle descriptions without throwing network errors.
- **Immutability Invariant**: Advisory AI explanations cannot mutate or overwrite the deterministic Rule Engine evaluation.

### 5. Visual Evidence Heatmap (Section F)
- **Coordinate Transformation**: Normalized coordinates (`0.0 .. 1.0`) from OCR bounding polygons are mathematically scaled to display pixel dimensions in [`EvidenceViewerScreen.tsx`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/apps/mobile/src/screens/EvidenceViewerScreen.tsx).
- **Coordinate Clamping**: All coordinates are clamped within `[0, 1]` to prevent rendering artifacts or clipping.
- **Honesty Invariant**: When an evidence region has no coordinates or empty bounding polygons, the UI explicitly states *"Region location unavailable"* rather than fabricating synthetic boxes.
- **Overlay Toggle**: Inspectors can toggle `HEATMAP ON/OFF` to view the raw package surface unobstructed.

### 6. LM-Vision Assessment Score (Section G)
- **Deterministic Formula**: Implemented in [`packages/rules/src/scoring/assessmentScore.ts`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/packages/rules/src/scoring/assessmentScore.ts) and exported from `@lm-vision/rules`:
  $$\text{Overall Score} = 0.40 \cdot S_{\text{mandatory}} + 0.25 \cdot S_{\text{mrp}} + 0.20 \cdot S_{\text{net\_qty}} + 0.15 \cdot S_{\text{evidence}}$$
- **Subscores Breakdown**:
  - $S_{\text{mandatory}}$: Compliance across general Rule 6(1) mandatory declarations.
  - $S_{\text{mrp}}$: Strict compliance with Rule 6(1)(e) format (*"inclusive of all taxes"*).
  - $S_{\text{net\_qty}}$: Compliance with standard metric SI units under Rule 12 / 13.
  - $S_{\text{evidence}}$: Image sharpness, illumination, and OCR extraction confidence.
- **Statutory Non-Binding Invariant**: The overall score is strictly an advisory operational index. A score of 85/100 does NOT pass a package if any individual statutory rule (e.g. MRP format) has a `FAIL` verdict.

### 7. Image Preprocessing: Dewarping & Glare Attenuation (Sections H & I)
- **Basic Geometric Dewarping**: Implemented in [`packages/perception/src/cv/dewarping.ts`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/packages/perception/src/cv/dewarping.ts). Provides basic 2D geometric rectification for cylindrical surfaces (bottles, cans, tubes).
- **Specular Glare Reduction**: Implemented in [`packages/perception/src/cv/glareReduction.ts`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/packages/perception/src/cv/glareReduction.ts). Attenuates specular reflections on glossy blister packs and metallic foils via adaptive luminance normalization.
- **Evidence Integrity**: Original photo bytes are preserved without modification as primary statutory evidence. Derived records receive distinct unique identifiers with full provenance metadata.

### 8. Hybrid Conflict Detection (Sections J & K)
- **Conflict Resolution Engine**: Located in [`packages/perception/src/hybrid/conflictDetector.ts`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/packages/perception/src/hybrid/conflictDetector.ts).
- **Discrepancy Identification**:
  - `VALUE_MISMATCH`: Local OCR observed value differs from Gemini observation (e.g. ₹150 vs ₹180).
  - `UNIT_MISMATCH`: Discrepancy in unit declarations (e.g. `g` vs `ml`).
- **Safety Invariant**: The system never silently chooses the AI or the OCR observation. Any conflict sets `status: 'CONFLICT'` and `requiresHumanVerification: true`, flagging the discrepancy for inspector review.

### 9. E-Commerce Declaration Comparison (Section L)
- **Comparison Screen**: Added [`EcommerceComparisonScreen.tsx`](file:///d:/antiprojects/LM-Vision/apps/mobile/src/screens/EcommerceComparisonScreen.tsx).
- **Inputs**: Supports manual field entry, pasted unstructured listing text, and raw catalog JSON.
- **Evaluated Fields**: Product Name, Maximum Retail Price (MRP), Net Quantity, Manufacturer / Packer, and Packaging Format.
- **Field Statuses**: `MATCH`, `MISMATCH`, and `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.
- **Legal Safeguard**: All mismatches are labeled objectively as field observations for inspector review, strictly avoiding criminal or fraud accusations.

### 10. Camera Lifecycle & UI Hardening (Sections N & Q)
- **Camera Flashlight State**: Managed by `useCameraLifecycle` and screen focus hooks. Torch is immediately disengaged on photo capture, screen blur, backgrounding, and navigation departure.
- **Text Overflow & Card Layouts**: All cards across results, review, findings, and rule screens utilize flexible containers (`flexWrap: 'wrap'`, `flexShrink: 1`, min-heights instead of fixed pixel heights) to ensure bilingual and lengthy manufacturer addresses render cleanly without clipping.

### 11. Security Audit & Architectural Cleanliness (Sections M & AB)
- **Client Security**: Verified that `@google/genai` is **not** imported in `apps/mobile/package.json`. No Gemini API keys or private credentials are embedded in the mobile binary. All remote AI requests route strictly through the backend AI Engine proxy.
